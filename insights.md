# 🎬 Key Insights from Movies Metadata Analysis

## 💰 Financial Findings
- Most movies don't make massive profits — only blockbusters skew the data
- Budget and revenue have a **strong positive correlation**
- High budget does NOT always mean high ROI

## 📈 Trends Over Time
- The **2000s–2010s** were Hollywood's most profitable decades
- Audience engagement (votes, popularity) grew significantly post-2000

## 🏆 Top Performers
- Avatar, Titanic & Star Wars dominate profit rankings
- Smaller films sometimes achieve **higher ROI** than big-budget movies

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib
